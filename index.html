Architecture

How It Works

From raw sensor data to Tesla dispatch in under 200 ms — four automated layers.

📡

Sense

Loop detectors, radar sensors, and probe-vehicle GPS report speed, volume, and occupancy from every highway segment every 30 seconds.

⚙️

Analyse

The Python engine applies BPR volume-delay, builds a weighted road graph, runs Dijkstra, and scores each segment for incidents vs. normal congestion.

🚦

Optimise

Adaptive signal timing and green-wave offsets are recalculated and pushed to the intersection controllers. VMS boards updated with diversion advice.

🚗

Dispatch

Reroutes streamed to Tesla FSD vehicles via Fleet API. AV units dispatched to incident zones. Passenger app shows updated ETA instantly.
